# Abin’s three-minute pitch

## 0:00–0:35 · The first click · 35 seconds

[ON SCREEN] Safari on the Mac, with the companion beside it. Start in Malayalam. Point out the language choices as you name them.

[SAY] I’m Abin. I built a companion for anyone who struggles with computers: your dad, your mom, and their dads and moms. You can read a letter and still have no idea that “Compose” means “write one.”

[SAY] It points. You click. Next time, you know where. I’ve tested Malayalam and English live. Hindi and Kannada are built too. Today I’ll show Malayalam. With Sarvam, more Indian languages are a small step.

## 0:35–1:10 · GasUndo · 35 seconds

[ON SCREEN] Open gasundo.live. Keep the district selector and restaurant search in view.

[SAY] Let’s try it on something built by two of this hackathon’s own organisers, Vaishakh Suresh and Viswanatha Kartha V. This is GasUndo! I’ve got a question for it.

[SPEAK TO COMPANION] ബെക്താഷ് കാക്കനാട് തുറന്നിട്ടുണ്ടോ?

[SAY] Is Bektash Kakkanad open? Watch the arrow. I pick Ernakulam. Now it’s pointing at “Search restaurants.”

[ON SCREEN] Select Ernakulam if needed. Click the search box, type “Bektash Kakkanad,” then click the matching suggestion. Let the companion read the report.

[SAY] There’s the catch. That report is old. Is it open today? We don’t know.

## 1:10–1:57 · YouTube · 47 seconds

[ON SCREEN] Switch to the real YouTube home page. Tap the mic.

[SPEAK TO COMPANION] ദർശന പാട്ട് പ്ലേ ചെയ്യൂ

[SAY] I asked for the Darshana song. There’s Search. I type Darshana, hit Enter, and the arrow picks out the official video.

[ON SCREEN] Click the official result. If a player ad appears, wait. Click Skip when the companion points to it. Once the song starts, lower the player volume so the next request is audible.

[SAY] Even if an ad comes up, it shows me Skip when I can use it. Now, let’s slow this down.

[SPEAK TO COMPANION] എനിക്ക് ഈ വീഡിയോ സ്ലോ ആക്കണം

[ON SCREEN] Follow Settings → Playback speed → “−”. Start at Normal. Click once per countdown prompt and give the last one time to update.

[SAY] Settings. Playback speed. See the little minus? Five, four, three, two, one. There. Three-quarter speed.

## 1:57–2:33 · Gmail · 36 seconds

[ON SCREEN] Switch to Abin’s real Gmail account, with private inbox rows out of view. Use a test address Abin controls and a short demo message.

[SPEAK TO COMPANION] എന്റെ മകന് ഒരു ഇമെയിൽ അയയ്ക്കണം

[SAY] “I want to email my son.” This is my real Gmail. There’s that word again. Compose. Now To, Subject, and here’s where I write my message.

[ON SCREEN] Click Compose. Enter the test address and press Return. Fill Subject and message. Skip the optional attachment if prompted. Pause on Send.

[SAY] The companion sees button and field names. It never reads my emails. My words, my click. Send.

[ON SCREEN] Click Send. Wait for Gmail’s “Message sent” notice and let Bulbul say “ഇമെയിൽ അയച്ചു”.

[SAY] Hear that? Gmail confirmed it went through.

## 2:33–2:55 · What comes next · 22 seconds

[ON SCREEN] Leave the completed step visible, then show the closing slide with “Next” above Android, Aadhaar and learning.

[SAY] That was Sarvam. Saaras turns speech into text. Sarvam’s language model understands the request. Bulbul talks back.

[SAY] Today it’s on Mac. Android is next. Imagine a grandparent updating their Aadhaar details. A student practising just the step they got stuck on. Imagine parents across India learning computers without having to learn English first.

## 2:55–3:00 · Close · 5 seconds

[ON SCREEN] Look up from the Mac. Give the last line to the room.

[SAY] Next time Amma calls, ask how she is. She’s already sent the email.

## Alternate closers · Pick one in place of the last line

[SAY] Dad used to ask, “Where do I click?” Next time he can show me.

[SAY] The best thing this arrow can do? Teach you where to click after it’s gone.

## Wi-Fi backup · 15 seconds in place of live narration

[ON SCREEN] Have a local recording ready before going on stage. If the connection drops, start the recording at the current site and rejoin the script at the next pointer cue.

[SAY] The Wi-Fi just dropped. Here’s a recording from my Mac of the same demo. Watch who’s doing the clicking. I ask in Malayalam, the arrow finds the button, and I take it from there.
