Companion: demo recording script

Colour key: YELLOW = what you say to the camera. PURPLE = what you say to the companion (mic). GREEN = what you click.
Malayalam is written in English letters. Read it as it sounds. The English meaning is next to it in brackets.

Before you hit record

  1. Safari: one window, three tabs: gasundo.live/?district=ernakulam, youtube.com, Gmail (inbox scrolled so that no private email is readable).
  2. Companion running. The panel sits beside Safari, set to Malayalam (or English; it understands both).
  3. YouTube speed on Normal, nothing playing. Mac volume about 60%. Do Not Disturb on.
  4. Click the mic once to start, speak, then click it again to stop.

1 · Intro ~35 sec

"I'm Abin. I built a companion for anyone who struggles with computers: your dad, your mom, and their dads and moms.

You can read a letter and still have no idea that Compose means write one.

It doesn't click for you. It teaches you. It points, you click, and next time you know where.

It's built for four languages: Malayalam, English, Hindi and Kannada. I've tested Malayalam and English live. With Sarvam, more Indian languages are a small step."

2 · GasUndo, the organisers' app ~45 sec

What GasUndo is (so you can say it in your own words): in March 2026 Kerala had a cooking-gas (LPG) shortage, and many restaurants closed or cut their menus. Two of this hackathon's organisers, Vaishakh Suresh and Viswanatha Kartha V, built gasundo.live ("gas undo?" means "is there gas?"). People crowd-reported which restaurants were open, had a limited menu, or were closed for the gas shortage, district by district. Onmanorama covered it. Today the reports are old, because the shortage is over.

"Let's start with something built by two of this hackathon's own organisers, Vaishakh and Viswanatha. This is GasUndo. During the gas shortage in March, it told people which restaurants were still open. I'll ask it in Malayalam."
Switch to the GasUndo tab (Ernakulam). Click the mic.
SAY TO COMPANION
"Bektash Kakkanad thurannittundo?"
(sounds like: bek-TAASH kaak-ka-NAAD thu-RAN-nit-tun-DO) = "Is Bektash Kakkanad open?"
English backup: "Is Bektash Kakkanad open?"
  1. The arrow points at Search restaurants. Click it and type Bektash.
  2. The arrow points at the suggestion Bektash Kakkanad. Click it.
  3. Let the companion read the report out loud.
"It found the restaurant and read the report: limited menu. And it says the report is old, so today's status is unknown. It won't make things up for Amma."

3 · YouTube ~45 sec

If you want to switch to English here: "My Malayalam isn't great, so I'll carry on in English. It understands both."

Switch to the YouTube tab. Click the mic.
SAY TO COMPANION
"Darshana paattu play cheyyoo"
(DAR-sha-na PAAT-tu play CHEY-yoo) = "Play the Darshana song"
English: "I want to watch Darshana"
  1. The arrow points at Search. Type darshana, then click the Search button it points at.
  2. The arrow points at the official Darshana video. Click it.
  3. If an ad plays, wait. Click Skip when the arrow points at it.
"It picked the official video, not a random channel. And if an ad comes up, it shows me Skip. Now let's slow it down."
Click the mic.
SAY TO COMPANION
"Enikku ee video slow aakkanam"
(e-NIK-ku ee video slow AAK-ka-nam) = "I want to slow this video down"
English: "Make it slower"
  1. Click Settings (the gear) when the arrow points at it.
  2. Click Playback speed.
  3. Click − each time it asks: 5, 4, 3, 2, 1. Give the last click a second.
"Settings. Playback speed. See the little minus? Five, four, three, two, one. Three-quarter speed. Three menus deep, one step at a time."

4 · Gmail ~40 sec

Switch to the Gmail tab. Click the mic.
SAY TO COMPANION
"Ente makanu oru email ayakkanam"
(EN-te MA-ka-nu o-ru email a-YAK-ka-nam) = "I want to send an email to my son"
English: "I want to send an email"
"This is my real Gmail. There's that word again: Compose."
  1. Click Compose.
  2. To: type your own address and press Return.
  3. Subject: type Hello. Message: type one line.
  4. If it offers Attach, click the panel's Skip button.
  5. Click Send.
"The companion sees only button and field names. It never reads my emails. My words, my click. Send."
It says "Email ayachu" (e-MAIL a-YA-chu) = "email sent".
"Hear that? Gmail confirmed it went through."

Today it runs on a Mac. Android is next.

Imagine a grandparent updating their Aadhaar details, guided step by step. A student learning computers at their own pace, in their own language. Parents across India learning computers without having to learn English first."

"Next time Amma calls, ask how she is. She's already sent the email."

Other endings: "Dad used to ask, 'Where do I click?' Next time, he can show me." · "The best thing this arrow can do? Teach you where to click after it's gone."

"One more thing. When our QA finds a bug, like 'make it faster' not being understood, it becomes a case. Devin opened a fix in about a minute, and we reran the same check on Devin's exact commit before I merged it."

If something breaks

  • The mic doesn't hear you: type the English line into the panel and press Ask.
  • The arrow doesn't appear on YouTube: move your mouse over the video (YouTube hides its buttons).
  • The Wi-Fi drops: "The Wi-Fi just dropped. Here's a recording of the same demo. Watch who does the clicking."
