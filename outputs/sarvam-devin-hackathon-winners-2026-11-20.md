Research checked 20 November 2026. For a Kerala solo build, the closest comparison is Jasim CM’s Kinkaran. Its demo gives the judge a voice order, a shop price list and an expected ₹178 receipt. For a documented Devin integration, Player Two has a video and a merged repair. For Sarvam, Sahayak has the original merchant onboarding recording and the speech configuration from the competition. ## H Company × 42AI Computer Use Agents Hackathon · Paris · 7–8 November

H Company and 42AI asked teams to demonstrate an agent operating real software. Cognition backed the event and offered €1,000 for Best Use of Devin. The host published 40 submissions. [Event and prizes](https://luma.com/hz9db5u9) · [Host’s results](https://fr.linkedin.com/company/h-company-ai) · [Organizer’s project gallery](https://showcase.hcompany.ai/?tab=h-42ai-hackathon#dex)

- **1st overall — H Companion**, Édouard Foussier, Matteo Atkinson and Tom Angles. Hold a key and ask for help. Holo locates the relevant control on the live screen, then highlights it or performs the task at the user’s chosen level of assistance. A separate read-only check looks for the expected result. The repository documents an Electron desktop client, Gladia speech recognition, a Python/FastAPI support backend and an optional Devin repair workflow for suspected defects in approved repositories. [Submission](https://showcase.hcompany.ai/p/gh-tooom123-h-company-computer-use-hackathon/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/Tooom123/H-Company_Computer-Use_Hackathon) · [Demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZL4C3UOBNY)
- **2nd overall — Rio**, Jacques Dumora, Talal Aamir and Wassim Badraoui. Turn a tutorial video or article into a lesson inside the software being taught. Holo checks the screen, an overlaid cursor shows where to click, and Gladia and Gradium handle spoken questions and answers. The Rust/Tauri app also has a Devin integration that converts supported successful runs into reusable scripts and requests test evidence. [Submission](https://showcase.hcompany.ai/p/gh-talal95c-hcompany-hack/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/talal95c/Hcompany-Hack) · [Devin integration](https://github.com/talal95c/Hcompany-Hack/blob/main/src-tauri/src/devin.rs) · [Demo video](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1FVefgwl89JevpZs0u09CWUI4ZdF73Z91/view)
- **3rd overall and Best Use of Devin — Player Two**, Dmitry Bogomolov. Holo plays Chocolate Doom from screenshots and reports a visual defect. A verifier replays the inputs and checks a hidden log. Once the developer approves a fix, the FastAPI service sends the finding, steps and log excerpt to Devin. Devin opens a PR against the game fork. The project rebuilds the game and repeats the test. The merged PR fixes a sky-rendering bug. The builder reports **7 minutes 18 seconds** from requesting the fix to verifying it in a run with a supplied test plan. A separate blind run found a wall-rendering defect. [Winner’s LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dmitry-bogomolov-4b81082b3_now-its-officially-my-lucky-sweater-among-activity-7525666148521107456-8iEq) · [GitHub and recorded results](https://github.com/kaktamsdengami/player-two) · [Merged Devin PR](https://github.com/kaktamsdengami/chocolate-doom/pull/4) · [Demo video](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1SIoFS1B4X_sixcLA1ScgdopQVdWiix_-/view)

## Devin Hacks Kochi · Kochi · 24–25 October

The event advertised 12 hours of solo building with Devin. Vaishakh Suresh and Viswanatha Kartha V were also listed as hosts of November’s HackaThoni. The organizer highlighted five projects. Two builders publicly identified their numbered placements. [Event brief](https://luma.com/3v8n74l1) · [Prize schedule](https://www.devinhacks.in/) · [Organizer’s recap](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:ugcPost:7521070219507707905/) · [HackaThoni hosts](https://luma.com/60zajedj)

- **1st — Woose**, Sunith V S. Sunith identifies an agentic AI video editor and shows the PS5 he won. His post uses **Woose**, while the organizer wrote **Wose**. The public footage shows the prize unboxing. A product walkthrough, winning repository, implementation stack and precise Devin contribution could not be verified. [Winner’s post and unboxing](https://x.com/truevibecoder/status/2116940632199512187) · [Organizer’s event footage](https://x.com/soulcastor3/status/2116550042152759341)
- **2nd — unresolved.** The published second prize was a Bambu Lab A1 Mini. The organizer also featured BetterNotch, Echo and a browser music project. The recap gives no second-place attribution. [Prizes](https://www.devinhacks.in/) · [Organizer’s recap](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:ugcPost:7521070219507707905/)
- **3rd — Kinkaran / Easy Bill**, Jasim CM. A Kerala shopkeeper sends a Telegram voice note in Malayalam, English or Manglish. Gemini extracts items and quantities. The app gets prices from the shop’s catalog, displays a UPI QR at the counter and returns a PDF receipt. The code uses Python, FastAPI, python-telegram-bot, ReportLab and a vanilla JavaScript PWA. The two-minute demo script gives a sample order and exact expected total. Jasim’s award post and event-night commit establish the submission. The public material does not specify Devin’s engineering tasks or include a verified product video. [Builder’s LinkedIn activity](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jasim-cm/recent-activity/all/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/jasimcm/kinkaran) · [Event-night commit](https://github.com/jasimcm/kinkaran/commit/6fc834a568aa1cb631887400739f3a0df3787cdf) · [Written demo script](https://github.com/jasimcm/kinkaran/blob/main/DEMO.md)

## AI Hackathon by LA Hacks · UCLA · 17–18 October

The organizer lists 425 participants and three Cognition **Best Use of Devin** awards. Devpost identifies all three projects as category winners; numbered placements are not established here. [Event and sponsor prizes](https://la-ai-hackathon-2026.devpost.com/) · [Results gallery](https://la-ai-hackathon-2026.devpost.com/project-gallery)

- **Booth Brief — sponsor-track winner**, Alan Roybal, solo. Even Realities G2 glasses show employer research and talking points. After the conversation, the wearer says the rep’s name and can cancel a follow-up by tilting their head. CREAO, Apollo, Deepgram, Node/TypeScript, AWS Lambda and Gmail power the workflow. Alan details Devin writing the lens app and backend, exercising each screen through the simulator, fixing physical-glasses issues and editing the presentation video. The event run sent mail to a test inbox. The source lives in the default branch’s `booth/` directory. [Submission and award](https://devpost.com/software/idk-idh1u0) · [GitHub](https://github.com/AlanRoybal/AILAHack) · [Demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VA6cJBguxKI)
- **Mulligan — sponsor-track winner**, Manas Dalvi and Advait Naik. AI agents act through an MCP gateway that records changes across Postgres, GitHub and Notion. The user can rewind a session while preserving later human edits. Outgoing payments and email can wait for iMessage approval. The team credits Devin with writing randomized undo tests that exposed three verification bugs and helping fix them. Devin can also connect as a protected client. Its gateway uses Python/FastAPI and the MCP SDK, its action log uses TimescaleDB, and its dashboard uses React/Vite/TypeScript. [Submission and award](https://devpost.com/software/mulligan) · [GitHub](https://github.com/Manas-33/mulligan) · [Merged test and fixes](https://github.com/Manas-33/mulligan/pull/17) · [Demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MK4Z1qSvnd0)
- **FlUni — sponsor-track winner**, Aiden Jeong, Lisa Zheng, Daniel Qi and Rio Okano Schillmoeller. A campus app combines proximity signals, symptom reporting and a heat map. A synthetic student population lets the team compare exposure models. The submission lists Swift, PyTorch, NumPy and Devin. It gives no task-level Devin attribution or numbered placement. Its GitHub Pages link returned 404, and a corresponding public repository could not be verified. [Submission and award](https://devpost.com/software/fluni) · [Demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gc5roVuGxDk)

## Paytm Build for India · Hyderabad · 22 August

At this one-day HackCulture edition, teams built for Paytm users and small businesses. The host and the winning consumer team name Sarvam as a partner. Two teams independently reported wins. [Host recap](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hackculture_last-weekend-we-brought-together-4700-activity-7497634643538788352-3QaQ) · [Event date and partnership](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sumit-ahuja-5652531a9_paytmaihackathon-buildforindia-ai-activity-7495454572275101696-f6mi)

- **Track 1, AI for Paytm Users — Pakka**, Team AI Hero Academia, Sagar Satapathy and Ranjan Nayak. It calculates available cash after obligations, warns about likely UPI AutoPay failures and flags risky QR recipients or mistyped UPI IDs. The team evaluated forecasts on a simulated transaction cohort. The code uses FastAPI and Next.js/React. Sarvam Bulbul v3 generates narration MP3s ahead of time, with optional Saaras v3 transcription for checking them. The risk scores come from the forecasting and payment-risk logic. A live prototype is public. A dedicated submission video could not be verified. [Sagar’s account](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sagar-satapathy-wishsagarks_buildforindia-paytm-aihackathon-activity-7497257838214356992-T7SW) · [Ranjan’s track confirmation](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ranjankn_buildforindia-paytmaihackathon-sarvamai-activity-7497953745369182208-Md4G) · [GitHub](https://github.com/wishsagarks/pakka-PAYtm-Engine) · [Voice implementation](https://github.com/wishsagarks/pakka-PAYtm-Engine/blob/main/scripts/gen_voice.py) · [Live demo](https://pakka-paytm-engine.vercel.app/paytm2)
- **Team Starfire reported a win — Paytm Intent Mesh**, Shoaib Sadiq Salehmohamed and Hansika Aredla. Their submission targets AI for Small Businesses. A customer speaks a cake order, budget and deadline. Sarvam transcribes and interprets the request. A rules-based router ranks nearby merchants, a simulated Soundbox receives offers and the customer completes a simulated UPI purchase. FastAPI, SQLite, server-sent events and 20 synthetic merchants support the demo. The public winner statement leaves the exact award and rank unspecified. [Builder’s result](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/shoaib-ssm_buildforindia-paytm-paytmaihackathon-activity-7497311009771192320-UTmh) · [GitHub](https://github.com/shoaib2000857/Paytm-AI-Hackathon-Hyderabad-Team-Starfire) · [Sarvam integration](https://github.com/shoaib2000857/Paytm-AI-Hackathon-Hyderabad-Team-Starfire/blob/main/backend/main.py) · [Recorded screenshot walkthrough](https://github.com/shoaib2000857/Paytm-AI-Hackathon-Hyderabad-Team-Starfire/blob/main/presentation-assets/vyapaar-mesh-walkthrough.mp4)

## Sarvam Epoch Buildathon · Bengaluru · 26 July build, 30 July finals

Sarvam announced the buildathon and finals. The judging recap identifies all three placements. [Sarvam’s announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sarvam-ai_introducing-the-sarvam-epoch-buildathon-activity-7484964198725271552-3-MO) · [Rankings and project descriptions](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/vinayakgavariya_buildwithsarvam-activity-7493728077878001664-BYc-)

- **1st — WireVoice**, Hithesh Karanth. An electrical technician asks about a wire or terminal in their own language and gets an answer that points to the exact schematic sheet. The public sources establish the workflow. The named Sarvam models, project repository and a dedicated product walkthrough remain unverified. [Judging recap](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/vinayakgavariya_buildwithsarvam-activity-7493728077878001664-BYc-) · [Builder interview](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/abhishek-reddy-t-a15400214_sarvam-sarvamepoch-sarvamai-activity-7494973830361448448-hmul)
- **2nd — Sahayak**, Akash Meruva and team, including Sachidananda Manideep. A Shopify merchant defines support policies and actions. Customers ask about products or orders by voice, and LangGraph routes each request to the store connector. The July configuration uses Saarika v2.5 for speech recognition, Saaras v2.5 for speech translation, Sarvam-105B, Sarvam-Translate v1 and **Bulbul v2** for speech output. The current README lists Bulbul v3. [Builder’s post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/akash-meruva-ab1420222_sarvamai-buildinpublic-hackathon-activity-7488923920335560704-C8R7) · [July GitHub source](https://github.com/akashmeruva9/sahayak-ucxp-sarvam/tree/e239560f5158fd9b1643c2aac4f6b9cb925ffcfb) · [July model settings](https://github.com/akashmeruva9/sahayak-ucxp-sarvam/blob/e239560f5158fd9b1643c2aac4f6b9cb925ffcfb/ai_engine/config.py) · [Merchant onboarding video](https://www.loom.com/share/c7904325fba347adaaad6f623799d98a) · [Full project video](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fa20573a-8d16-46e7-bd67-376eea119c84)
- **3rd — SADAK**, Team Dark Chocolate. Fahim Ahmed names Parth Mittal and Mardav Gandhi in his results post. Players practice everyday errands in a 3D scene, such as hailing an auto or ordering from a food stall. They speak, hear the character reply and get feedback. The July source integrates Saaras v3 speech recognition, Sarvam-105B for conversation and Bulbul v3 for speech output. [Builder’s result](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ahmedfahim21_we-came-2nd-runner-up-at-the-sarvam-epoch-activity-7490255210649452544-RCMg) · [GitHub](https://github.com/mittal-parth/sadak) · [Competition-period integration](https://github.com/mittal-parth/sadak/blob/ebacadea683a8fa86679c8ab1465ae7045d43535/game_engine/lib/sarvam.ts) · [Final-checkpoint video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gadi-osujrs)

The event-era app dependencies add useful context: Sahayak uses Expo and React Native for its client; SADAK uses Next.js, React and Three.js for its browser game.
